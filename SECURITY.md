## Reporting a Vulnerability

How to report a vulnerability.

Where to go: security.espeezy.com / support.espeezy.com

Expect to get an update on a
reported vulnerability within 24 hrs of reporting.


What to expect if the vulnerability is accepted or
declined, etc : Email confirmation.
